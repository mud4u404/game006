# 任务 00：风格锚点：雷恩表情表 A

**目的**：先只画主角一张，把画风定下来。这张通过以后，后面每张图都把它作为「风格参考」附上，整套美术才会统一。

## 附件
无。只用文字设定，由 ChatGPT 自由设计。这张通过后就是雷恩的「官方形象」，游戏里的配色反过来按它调整。

## 发给 ChatGPT 的话（整段复制）

```text
Expression sheet A for Rein, the main hero. Follow the project style guide exactly.
Canvas 1024x1536 (portrait), 2x2 grid, transparent background.
Panels (left to right, top to bottom): neutral, gentle smile, angry, sad.

Rein: 17-year-old squire knight. Spiky dark chestnut-brown hair with a cowlick, a few strands over the forehead.
Warm amber eyes, earnest and stubborn. Lean build.
Crimson tabard over light silver armor with rounded pauldrons, a cream wool scarf around the neck.
A small glowing ember-orange scale-shaped birthmark on the left side of his neck, partly hidden by the scarf.
```

## 审图标准（Claude 审核时按这几条）
- [ ] 是 2×2 四格，每格同一个人、同样大小，格子之间有空隙，没有边框和文字
- [ ] 身体朝画面右侧（3/4 侧身）
- [ ] 不是 Q 版，脸不幼态；看起来像 17 岁的见习骑士
- [ ] 深栗色刺头、琥珀色眼睛、红色罩袍 + 银甲 + 米色围巾、左颈有发光鳞片胎记
- [ ] 背景透明（或纯白）；不是灰白棋盘格

## 不满意时，在同一个对话里接着说（可直接复制）

| 问题 | 发这句 |
| --- | --- |
| 太幼/太萌 | `Make him look older and more grounded, less cute; sharper jaw, smaller eyes, more mature anime style.` |
| 画风太写实/太厚涂 | `Push it more toward clean anime cel shading with crisp lineart; less painterly rendering on the face.` |
| 朝向错了 | `Turn the body to three-quarter view facing the viewer's right in all four panels.` |
| 四格大小不一致 | `Keep exactly the same framing, head size and position in all four panels.` |
| 画了棋盘格背景 | `Use a real transparent background, or a flat pure white background. No checkerboard.` |
| 颜色跑偏 | `Keep the colors: dark chestnut hair, amber eyes, crimson tabard, silver armor, cream scarf.` |

拿不准就直接把图发到和 Claude 的对话里，由 Claude 审核。通过后下载原图，**文件名改成 `portrait_rein_a.png`**，按 `art/README.md` 第 3 步上传。
